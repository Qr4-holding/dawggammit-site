Add from KoolTeez

Paste product links or a whole collection link, one per line. Each product keeps its KoolTeez address, so its Buy now button on DawgGammit.com goes straight back to the same product page.

The server opens each KoolTeez page and reads the name, price and photos. It sorts the photos itself: the plain product shot goes in the shop grid, and a photo of someone wearing it (if there is one) is offered for the lookbook. A collection link brings in every product in it; allow about a second per product.

On DawgGammit.com

Use ↑ ↓ to set the order on the site, and the product line menu to group a product (Hoodies, Sweatshirts, Mugs and so on). A product line appears in the site's menu and Product filter as soon as it has a visible product. The eye hides a product without deleting it.

Lookbook: Worn by outlaws

These photos fill the lookbook on the site, shown in full so heads and boots are never cut off. Each photo links to the product it shows. Take one from a product's KoolTeez photos, or upload your own; tall photos (9:16, like a phone screen) fit best.

Ordering: new photos are added first. Drag a photo to a new place, or use the buttons under it. The number on each photo is its position on the site; the site shows the first 12. Changes save as a draft; press Publish to put them live.
